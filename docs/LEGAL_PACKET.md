# Legal Packet — Items to Send to Counsel

Purpose: provide counsel with a concise set of questions and information for reviewing Pollacle as the platform evolves toward a new community-support and partnership-based model.

## 1) Product summary

- Pollacle is a fundraising and community-support platform designed to help creators, nonprofits, schools, teams, clubs, and community organizations receive meaningful support.
- Pollacle is transitioning away from its original survey-based support model.
- The original survey/market-research model is no longer the active product direction.
- Current development is focused on more reliable community-support opportunities, partnerships, sponsorships, and other ways to help campaigns reach their goals.
- Public fundraiser creation is currently limited while the new support model is being developed.
- Any future third-party partner integration should be reviewed before launch and should be clearly disclosed to users.

## 2) Data flows

- Users may sign in through OAuth, where enabled, and Pollacle may store basic account information such as email address and name.
- Fundraiser creators may provide campaign information, including titles, stories, categories, goals, and related public content.
- Public campaign pages may display fundraiser information and campaign activity.
- Pollacle may collect technical information such as IP address, browser/device information, referrer information, and similar operational data.
- Cookie and consent functionality may record consent information through the Pollacle consent system.
- Third-party services may process information as necessary to provide authentication, hosting, analytics, advertising, security, communications, or future support opportunities.
- Data retention periods should be reviewed and established for each category of information.

## 3) Legal documents requested

- Privacy Policy — review for applicable U.S. state privacy laws and any other jurisdictions served.
- Terms of Service — review fundraising disclosures, user responsibilities, prohibited activities, third-party services, and platform limitations.
- Fundraiser/creator terms or supplemental rules, if appropriate.
- Partner and sponsorship agreements for future business relationships.
- Appropriate disclosures for any third-party offers, sponsorships, rewards, or support opportunities introduced to the platform.
- Any fundraising-specific legal disclosures required by applicable jurisdictions.

## 4) Security & compliance questions

- What privacy and consumer-protection requirements apply to Pollacle's new support and partnership model?
- What disclosures are required when a third-party company provides an offer, sponsorship, promotion, or other support opportunity?
- What contractual protections should Pollacle require from future partners?
- What liability, indemnification, intellectual-property, and data-processing provisions should be included in partner agreements?
- What age restrictions, age-gating, or parental-consent requirements apply?
- What additional disclosures are appropriate if Pollacle receives compensation from partners?
- Are there state-specific charitable fundraising, commercial co-venture, or solicitation rules that could apply to Pollacle or participating campaigns?
- What records should Pollacle maintain to demonstrate transparency and compliance?

## 5) Business-model review

Counsel should review the final partner-based model before public launch, including:

- How Pollacle is compensated by partners.
- How partner funds or sponsorship amounts are allocated.
- Whether funds are paid directly to campaigns, organizations, creators, or another entity.
- Whether Pollacle is acting as a fundraising platform, marketplace, advertising platform, commercial co-venturer, or another type of service.
- Any required disclosures regarding Pollacle's relationship with partners.
- Any tax, reporting, payment-processing, or consumer-protection obligations created by the final model.

## 7) Important development note

Pollacle's original survey-based support system has been retired from the active application. The replacement partner-based support model is still under development and should not be represented publicly as fully operational until the applicable partner relationships, business terms, disclosures, and technical implementation have been finalized.

Use this packet as a starting point for legal review. It is not a substitute for advice from qualified legal counsel.
