------------------

Building **Pollacle.com** started with a simple mission: to create a reliable platform that provides meaningful ways for people to support fundraisers and their communities. We wanted to give people a straightforward way to contribute their time and opinions instead of their money.

For a while, third-party programmatic survey routers seemed like the easiest way to make that happen.

But as we tested the system and listened to user feedback, we became increasingly frustrated with the experience it created. The reality we saw did not align with the kind of platform we want Pollacle to become.

So we believe it is time to be transparent about what we learned, why we are changing direction, and what comes next.

## The Honest Truth About the Paid Survey Struggle

If you have tried taking surveys on Pollacle recently, you may have experienced some of the same frustrations we encountered while testing traditional survey systems.

### The Disqualification Trap

One of the biggest problems is being screened out of a survey after spending significant time answering questions.

From a user's perspective, spending 10 or 15 minutes providing information only to discover that the survey is no longer available or that you do not qualify can make the experience feel like a waste of time.

That is not the experience we want Pollacle to provide.

### Delayed and Retroactive Rejections

Another frustration with traditional survey systems is that rewards may depend on processes outside of the user's control.

In some cases, survey responses can be reviewed after completion, creating uncertainty around whether a user will ultimately receive credit.

For a platform built around respecting people's time, that uncertainty is a serious problem.

### A Broken User Experience

The result can become a cycle of:

**Start a survey → answer questions → get screened out → start another survey → repeat.**

When that happens too often, the experience stops feeling empowering and starts feeling frustrating.

During our testing, we saw success rates that were simply not good enough for the kind of platform we want to build.

We refuse to build Pollacle around an experience where users feel that their time is not being respected.

## The Pivot: A User-First Model

Because our priority is protecting your time and creating more predictable ways to contribute, **we are officially moving away from traditional automated survey widgets.**

This does not mean Pollacle is going away.

It means we are changing how we approach the problem.

We are reimagining how Pollacle can generate revenue and turn that value into meaningful support for fundraisers and participants.

Here are some of the alternatives we are actively exploring.

### 1. Direct Community Sponsorships

We want to build relationships directly with trusted brands, local businesses, organizations, and matching donors.

Instead of relying entirely on automated survey routing, a sponsor could support a specific Pollacle activity with a clearly defined reward.

The goal is simple:

**A clearly defined action should have a clearly defined reward.**

### 2. Transparent, Goal-Based Tasks

We are exploring verified tasks where completion can be tracked programmatically.

For example, a task could have a clearly defined starting point, completion requirement, and reward.

If you complete the required action, the platform should be able to recognize that completion without sending you through an endless chain of survey screen-outs.

### 3. Dedicated Partner Spotlights

We would rather provide a small number of worthwhile opportunities than overwhelm users with thousands of questionable or low-success opportunities.

That means hand-selecting opportunities from partners who genuinely want to participate in the Pollacle community.

Quality matters more than quantity.

## Moving Forward Together

Pollacle is not going anywhere.

But the way we value your time is changing.

This pivot gives us an opportunity to move away from a system that has produced too much uncertainty and toward an ecosystem built around transparency, reliability, and trust.

We are still early.

That means we have the opportunity to learn from what did not work and build something better before Pollacle grows larger.

We want to thank everyone who has tested Pollacle, completed surveys, created fundraisers, provided feedback, or simply followed along with the project.

Your feedback helped us identify these problems early.

Now we are going to use what we learned to build the next version of Pollacle.

**The goal has not changed: give people another way to make an impact.**

The way we get there is changing.

Stay tuned for our upcoming sponsorship and partner opportunities. We're excited to show you what a truly user-first Pollacle can look like.
